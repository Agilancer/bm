BELL MOURN — TEN RARE WIZARD SETS

Rare is visual art direction; game item rarity must be assigned on import.
Each set has all five gear pieces. Geometry remains the supplied wizard.
Each original set ZIP contains its recipe, assets and four-angle preview.

jadecoil_rare — Jadecoil Custodian — moss palette: Jade scale silk, antique gold, dark verdigris guards and leaf crests.
sable_orbit_rare — Sable Orbit — night palette: Ink-blue celestial velvet, pewter guards and warm electrum trim.
rosequartz_rare — Rosequartz Savant — amethyst palette: Dusty rose floral velvet, plum guards and polished silver.
ivory_thicket_rare — Ivory Thicket — ash palette: Ivory botanical jacquard, forest guards and aged gold edging.
carmine_eclipse_rare — Carmine Eclipse — crimson palette: Deep carmine star silk, charcoal guards and cold silver crests.
lapis_conclave_rare — Lapis Conclave — night palette: Royal lapis brocade, bronze guards and rich gold latticework.
malachite_court_rare — Malachite Court — moss palette: Olive geometric brocade, champagne guards and gilded leaf insignia.
violet_cinder_rare — Violet Cinder — amethyst palette: Violet pinstripe silk, petrol guards and rose-gold flame embroidery.
