BELLMOURN — TEN EPIC WIZARD SETS

Epic is visual art direction; game item rarity must be assigned on import.
Each set has all five gear pieces. Geometry remains the supplied wizard.
Each original set ZIP contains its recipe, assets and four-angle preview.

ossuary_regent_epic — Ossuary Regent — ash palette: Charcoal funerary brocade, aged bone skulls and ivory edging.
bloodwake_lich_epic — Bloodwake Lich — crimson palette: Blackened wine velvet, copper-bone skulls and thorn embroidery.
venomcrypt_epic — Venomcrypt Hierophant — moss palette: Poison-green scales, pale jade skulls and serpentine brocade.
voidbone_oracle_epic — Voidbone Oracle — amethyst palette: Deep violet orbital silk, cold lilac skulls and blackened guards.
drowned_reliquary_epic — Drowned Reliquary — night palette: Abyssal blue tide embroidery, sea-bone skulls and tarnished silver.
heliodor_sovereign_epic — Heliodor Sovereign — ash palette: Champagne solar damask, heavy gold guards and sunburst crests.
peacock_astromancer_epic — Peacock Astromancer — night palette: Peacock blue eye-pattern silk, violet guards and electrum edging.
briar_opal_epic — Briar Opal — moss palette: Sage floral damask, pale opal guards and intricate champagne trim.
phoenix_carmine_epic — Phoenix Carmine — crimson palette: Carmine feather brocade, burnished amber guards and rich gold flames.
amaranth_celestarch_epic — Amaranth Celestarch — amethyst palette: Amaranth crystal brocade, blue-silver guards and platinum crests.
