## Rule breaks both saw (fix first, everywhere)
- A pilaster crossed by a string course or dado is a cross at every bay: loud on cream (ch_court) and on blue (tp_long,
  tp_salon), weaker in cd_vault, cd_scriv, ch_treas, ch_crypt, da_flood. Stop the course at each pilaster.
- Chandeliers: a rod and spokes in a ring, or a chain on through the ring to a lamp below, is a cross in a ring from under
  it. Three chains to the rim, no hub, nothing hung through it.
- Banner poles with a yard across the top carrying two flags (p2_mile, sv_rookeries, sv_cinder): a T. One arm from the
  pole's head, one flag; no buff beside red.
- tn_tavern: a window with mullion and transom (four panes); check the shutter's bracing (an X?) in sv_rookeries.

## Every interior (both)
- Every room is the same giant beamed box with thirty orange points. Give each room its own section (tall and narrow, low,
  a shaft with galleries, a drum), three or four pools of light with dark between, one accent colour a zone.
- Rooms are three times too big for what's in them: shrink them or break them up with things that stop the eye.
- A room must have its noun: a treasury with treasure, a mirror gallery with mirrors, a crypt with tombs.
- One hero piece seen from each way in.
- The gates between delves are the same blue ring in a brick slab everywhere: dress each.

## The castle outside

shafts; bridges with springers and braces; a find and a real view on each deck; red slit windows and hangings so it isn't a
black cut-out at ten in the morning; the stair lit; the Needles' heads.
