# Style guide: City State Phobos and Castle Velscour

## The place

Phobos is an old walled city ruled by the demon lord **Velscour**, whose castle stands on a crag at its head with his
**Keep** on top. Its people are poor, watched and afraid; three bands of quiet resistance hide in its yards. It's late
medieval in build (stone, brick, timber, slate, lead, iron), soot-dark and damp, with bone used as a material the
closer you get to the castle. Think grim, real, weathered and lived in: not cartoon, not glossy, not clean fantasy.

## The districts, and how they differ (by hue and stone size, not just brightness)

| Where | Feel | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| The Marches (outside) | old keeps, lichen | cool lichen grey |
| City wall, Barbican | huge dark blocks | blue-black basalt, bone joints high up |
| Rookeries, Tanners' Steps, Wharf (poor) | smoky brick, daub, tar | smoky brick, tannery ochre |
| Cinder Quarter (burnt) | char, ash, soot | ash grey, black |
| Chainmarket, Barracks, King's Mile | small warm blocks, clay brick | warm brown-grey, clay red |
| Bone Yards, Ossuary | bone, grave rows | bone grey, ivory |
| Mages' Rise, Dukes' Row (rich) | big tight ashlar, damask | cold violet-grey, wine |
| Castle Velscour (three wards) | curtain granite, iron | cold steel blue-grey |
| The Keep | oxblood-black basalt netted with ivory bone joints | oxblood, black, ivory, plum |

**Velscour's colour is blood red.** His forces wear palette swaps by wing: blood red (his own), royal blue, white
with skulls, forest green, grey with gold. Royal blue and grey-with-gold are never shown side by side.

## Always

- Photoreal, tactile, weathered: real stone grain, mortar, wear on edges, grime where water and hands go.
- Muted, desaturated palettes; colour from materials (rust, moss, dye, clay), never from the light.
- Flat even light, straight-on, filling the frame.
- Signs of age and use: chips, patches, repairs, stains, soot above, damp below.

## Never

- Text, letters, numbers, runes, monograms, signatures, watermarks, logos.
- Crosses, sunbursts, haloes, bells, saints, angels, doves, chalices, or any holy or church sign (that is the other
  side's; this city belongs to Velscour). Pointed Gothic shapes are fine; Christian symbols are not.
- Sunny, warm stock-photo looks: patio paving, bathroom tile, terracotta-and-sun, beige render, cheerful colours.
- Baked lighting: sun patches, cast shadows, vignettes, highlights, ambient-occlusion gradients.
- Perspective, sky, scenery, people, objects lying on a tile unless the prompt asks for them.
- Gore beyond old bones and old dried blood.
- A shape that repeats noticeably (one big lump, a zigzag, a single odd stone).

## Velscour's own signs (allowed, wanted on his things)

Horned skulls; a crown of thorns; thorn branches and briars; bat wings; great curling horns; small skulls in rows.
Carved, woven or painted: never as letters or as a monogram.
