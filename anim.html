Third-Person Gaits

Old beside new, each on a floor running at the game's pace. Red is how it was; green is how it is now. A floor that keeps pace with the feet means no sliding.
The game moves you at 5.6 m/s and 8.6 m/s holding Shift. The old run was made for 4.2 m/s, so it was played too fast and slid. Each new gait is made in Blender for its own pace (tools/anim/gait.py), a loop of eight steps with no two the same.
